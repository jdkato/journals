# A Novel Randomized Trial Of Zetaprol Versus Placebo In Adults With Hypertension: The XYZ Study

## Abstract

Hypertension is very common. We tested a novel drug in a randomised trial. 120 patients were enrolled. The data was analysed with ANOVA and the effect was significant [1]. Blood pressure fell 12mmHg (p=0.000).

## Introduction

Prior work (Smith et al., 2020) proves that zetaprol works in order to lower pressure. It is groundbreaking. See the figure below.

## Materials and methods

Patients were recruited in 2023. Doses were 5mg or 10mg given at 37 degrees C. We utilize SPSS for analysis. Ages of 18-65 years were allowed.
